PlatformFlow updates

1. Animated live 3D-style background retained (CSS animated perspective grid and moving glow).
2. Platform allocation checks occupancy intervals and avoids assigning the same platform to overlapping trains at the same station.
3. Station Master approval rejects a platform choice when it overlaps another train's allocated interval.
4. Existing approved conflicts are flagged for review and their valid platform/time intervals remain reserved, preventing new trains from being allocated on top of them until the conflict is resolved.
5. Rake-sharing rows show the arriving and departing service's independently assigned platform number when those services exist in the selected station plan. Otherwise, each service is shown as Not assigned. A rake link itself does not assign platforms.

Important: This is a planning prototype. Platform counts, train times, rake relationships, clearance buffers, and every proposed assignment must be checked against authoritative railway data and operational rules before real-world use.
