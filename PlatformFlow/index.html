Movement planning rules

Configurable prototype estimates—not real operational instructions.

01

Originating

Prefer the last two platforms. Default planned platform-entry target: 30 minutes before the entered planned time.

02

Destination

Prefer the last three platforms. Default maintenance-movement estimate: 20 minutes after the entered arrival time.

03

Intermediate

Use normal platform allocation and the entered occupancy duration. A 5-minute planning buffer is applied between occupancy intervals.
